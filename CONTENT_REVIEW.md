# Full-catalog content handoff

The catalog contains 2,000 unique lemma entries, ten levels of 200, and twenty categories. Every entry has a local, decoded image with creator, license and source metadata: 1,705 ARASAAC pictograms, 66 Commons photographs and 229 original concept diagrams. There are 1,770 distinct image files; related entries sometimes share an appropriate picture.

All 2,000 entries were inspected in forty contact sheets. Another 122 pictures were corrected or replaced and checked in three correction sheets. The per-entry integrity, provenance and review record is in data/image_review.json; data/image_corrections.json lists the corrections. No image is automatically approved or published. English meanings, grammar, pictures and abstract associations still require family review.

Before children begin, prepare the first twenty cards: confirm the chosen English sense and image, enter your Libyan Arabic spelling and accepted variants, record your pronunciation, approve and publish. Then prepare the next batch. No family dialect or parent voice has been fabricated. Mock Arabic and silent recordings exist only in isolated tests.

Parent preview includes drafts across all levels without changing child progress. Children explore only ready cards, in random category batches across all levels; exploration practice remains separate from homework.

Parents can move words after learning begins. A move swaps destination positions, keeping 200 entries per level. Previously issued homework and quiz snapshots remain unchanged. Future assignments follow a rebalanced per-child plan that avoids assigning the same entry twice.

ARASAAC assets require attribution, noncommercial use and share-alike. Commons photographs carry their individual licenses. Original diagrams and adapted curriculum data use CC BY-SA 4.0. Keep attribution when backing up or distributing lesson materials.

Parents can add multiple optional spoken examples per word, with optional labels. Children may listen without completing anything. Examples are excluded from quizzes, scoring, publication readiness and homework requirements. Use the separate pronunciation recorder for the quiz word itself.
